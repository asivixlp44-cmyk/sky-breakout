Short Description
Run, jump and break out through 135 stages in the sky! Nine worlds, harder all the way, and Storm Rush races against other players. Earn Stars, gear, trails and buddies.
Description
One long obby through the sky, 135 stages and nine worlds, harder all the way: pool toys over a turquoise lagoon, candy clouds, mossy ruins above the jungle, a neon arcade at dusk, a clockwork yard of swinging hammers, slippery frost peaks, a rift of fire over a lava sea, platforms in orbit among the stars and a golden crown of fire. Every stage starts on a checkpoint and every new stage pays Coins, and each world ends in a gauntlet with nowhere to save. Jump gaps and pillars, ride sliding platforms and spinning discs, outrun crumbling bridges, hop stone rollers, leap between pumping pistons, weave through flame jets, board platforms in orbit, bounce on spring pads, climb ladders up truss towers, dodge hammers, time the laser curtains, guess the right door and stay on the safe tiles. Reach the Breakout Balloon at the end to claim a Star, then run it again for bigger pay. Stuck on a stage? Skip it with Gems you earned. Every few minutes everyone is invited to a Storm Rush: eight racers on one course with a storm rolling up behind them, longer and harder as you level up. Buy gear with the Coins you earn, hatch buddies out of eggs to follow you, and show off trails bought with your Stars. Daily rewards, daily tasks, quests, FREE gifts, codes and leaderboards. Everything is earned in the game: there is nothing to buy with Bux.

Hosting steps

  1. GitHub: create the empty repo LEGiON-Platforms/sky-breakout (no README) and add the repo secret LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN.
  2. hosting.bloxity.io: the app sky-breakout must exist (its id is GAME_ID in .github/workflows/deploy.yml).
  3. Push the code to main (as asivixlp44-cmyk): the workflow builds the server image and the client and deploys both.
  4. Check https://sky-breakout.host.bloxity.io/health (it should say "db": true: Legion provides MONGODB_URI) and play at https://sky-breakout.play.bloxity.io.
  5. Create the game in Manage Games with the values above (slug exactly sky-breakout).
